# Proposed NZAVS collaboration: baseline imagery and subsequent psychological distress

Lead: [name, role, affiliation]. Academic sponsor: [name and institution, if applicable]. Draft for a feasibility enquiry; not submitted or approved.

**Question.** Does Time 13 visual-imagery vividness have a prospective association with Time 14 psychological distress beyond baseline distress, auditory imagery and prespecified demographic factors?

**Contribution.** The 2026 NZAVS imagery paper reports concurrent associations. This proposal tests a temporally ordered association with the same outcome at follow-up, while accounting for its baseline level. It does not estimate a causal effect of imagery and does not presume that imagery was repeatedly measured.

**Requested feasibility confirmation.** Are T13 visual/auditory imagery, T13/T14 K6, and baseline demographics linkable for a new collaboration? What are the matched counts overall and among T13 respondents reporting no visual imagery? Is this question already underway within the team? Which access, institutional sponsorship and analysis-governance requirements apply?

**Primary endpoint and analysis.** T14 K6, modelled using baseline K6, seven-category T13 visual imagery, auditory imagery, age and age-squared, recorded sex/gender, the recommended socioeconomic measure and elapsed follow-up time. Use survey-design-appropriate estimation where applicable. The primary visual-imagery test is a joint test across categories, with an adjusted outcome profile. A prespecified secondary contrast compares no visual imagery with the source study's typical-imagery category. Report original-scale mean differences with uncertainty.

**Secondary endpoint.** T14 Personal Wellbeing Index with baseline PWI adjustment, if measured comparably in both waves. T12 prior outcomes and T15 follow-up are optional sensitivity/extension variables, subject to availability and team approval. Rumination mediation is outside the first paper's core scope.

**Missingness.** Request retention information for all eligible baseline respondents and applicable weights. Evaluate complete cases, panel-appropriate imputation and response-weight sensitivity without double-counting existing weight adjustments. Include sensitivity to non-random follow-up missingness.

**Requested extract.** Anonymized panel ID; T13 imagery; baseline/follow-up K6; completion wave/timing; age; recorded sex/gender; socioeconomic measure; eligibility/retention indicators; relevant weights/design fields. Secondary PWI and optional prior/later outcomes only as justified above. No names, participant contact information, exact addresses or unnecessary clinical records are requested.

**Outputs.** One preregistered observational paper, reproducible analysis code, sample/retention flow, adjusted imagery–distress estimates and sensitivity analyses. Data and outputs remain subject to NZAVS permissions. Public reporting of participant-level data is not presumed.

**Collaboration.** We would agree the protocol, overlap with existing projects, storage/access arrangements and substantive collaborator roles with the NZAVS team before transfer or analysis. The detailed analysis plan may be refined during the feasibility audit, before examining the proposed prospective associations.

Reference: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2026.109527. Official collaboration/documentation route: https://www.psych.auckland.ac.nz/en/about/new-zealand-attitudes-and-values-study.html.
