H1 to H2b are the three main tests. They were run in a fixed order and each looked for a difference in the predicted direction only (one-sided p). S1 to S4 are the secondary tests. Their p values are corrected for running five tests (Holm's method), so a test can have a 95% confidence interval that excludes zero, as S2 does, and still not be significant after correction. All models adjust for age and sex. SD: difference in comparison-group standard deviations. OR: odds ratio, how many times higher the odds of absent imagery were in the autism group. Number of people: 898 for H1 to H2b, S1 and S3; 982 for S2; 832 for S4.
