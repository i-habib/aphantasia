The first row compares percentages with Fisher's exact test. The other rows are differences between the autism and comparison groups, adjusted for age and sex, with 95% confidence intervals. Negative values mean the autism group scored lower. A percentile difference compares the groups at the same point in their range of scores: the 10th percentile compares the lowest-scoring 10% of each group, and the 50th percentile (median) compares the middle of each group. These were estimated with quantile regression. Each dataset uses its own six senses and is measured against its own comparison group; Arnold et al.'s sixth sense is bodily sensation rather than movement. Groups: New et al., 150 autism and 749 comparison (898 with age recorded); Arnold et al., 327 and 327. These analyses were not part of the registered plan.
