# Terminology list

Check the final PDF against this list. Each term should be defined in words where it first appears and used the same way afterwards.

| Term | Meaning | First defined |
|---|---|---|
| Autism group | Respondents who ticked autism after a question combining diagnosis and strong suspicion. Includes 46 who also ticked ADHD. | Methods, Participants |
| ADHD | Attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder. Expand at first use in the introduction too. | Methods, Participants |
| ADHD-only group | Ticked ADHD but not autism. | Methods, Participants |
| Comparison group | Reported no neurodevelopmental condition. Never "controls" or "healthy". | Methods, Participants |
| Absent imagery | The lowest possible score in a sense: "no experience" on all five items (5 of 35 in New et al.; 0 of 50 in Arnold et al.). A questionnaire answer, not a test of ability. | Methods, Imagery measure |
| Top score | The highest possible score in a sense (35; 50). | Methods, Exploratory analyses |
| Overall imagery score | Average of the six sense scores, each expressed in comparison-group standard deviations, then rescaled the same way. | Methods, Imagery measure |
| SD | Standard deviation. In results, always the comparison group's SD. | Methods, Imagery measure |
| Six senses | Sight, sound, smell, taste, touch and movement in New et al.; bodily sensation replaces movement in Arnold et al. | Methods, Imagery measure / Comparison |
| Five-sense check | Exploratory re-analysis without the sixth sense. | Methods, Comparison |
| Registered / exploratory | Registered: in the OSF plan before the comparisons were run. Exploratory: added after the registered results were known. | Methods, Data and registration |
| QMI | Questionnaire upon Mental Imagery; New et al. used an extended version. | Methods, Imagery measure |
| PSI-Q | Plymouth Sensory Imagery Questionnaire, used by Arnold et al. | Methods, Comparison |
| AQ | Autism Spectrum Quotient, a self-report autistic-trait questionnaire. | Methods, Comparison |
| HC3 standard errors | Standard errors that do not assume equal variance across groups. | Methods, Registered analyses |
| Firth regression | Penalised logistic regression that gives finite estimates when a predictor perfectly predicts the outcome. | Methods, Registered analyses |
| Fixed order (primary tests) | Each later primary prediction counts only if all earlier ones were supported. | Methods, Registered analyses |
| Holm correction | Adjustment of p values across the five secondary tests. | Methods, Registered analyses |
| Quantile regression | Compares groups at a given percentile instead of the mean. | Methods, Exploratory analyses |
| Odds ratio (OR) | Ratio of the odds of absent imagery between groups. Not a risk ratio. | Results, Table 2 |

Avoid: "chemosensory", "domain", "component", "floor/ceiling" in the main text (use "smell and taste", "sense", "absent imagery", "top score"). "Domain" may appear only in the supplement's model descriptions.
