Why these analyses were run. Attention check: to see whether careless responding explains the difference. Emotion, voice and inner-body imagery: the three kinds of imagery New et al. measured that are not in the overall score. Treatment and education: possible confounders recorded in the survey. Empathy self-rating: the survey's only non-imagery 1-7 rating, used to check whether autistic respondents simply rate themselves lower on any 1-7 item; adjusting for it reduced the difference from -0.42 to -0.34. AQ 26 or more: Arnold et al.'s own selection. AQ without imagination items: whether absent imagery goes with autistic traits beyond the items that ask about imagination (Bouyer et al., 2025). Dance, Ward and Simner (2021): a public dataset with AQ and six-sense imagery in people with and without aphantasia (OSF h24vm; their AQ total omits item 3). The pooled estimate uses fixed-effect inverse-variance weights across two questionnaires whose sixth senses differ, and it selects people by their scores; the +/-0.2 SD bound is a conventional 'small' effect, chosen after the results were known.

## Reproduction and checks

All 156 registered estimate rows were reproduced exactly in a separate run (largest absolute difference 0.0). The frozen plan's SHA-256 is ce102d64229b6a4efb2a7c646048f17c5ab338829f73bed4121bcc1deb147c14, and the original output files were unchanged.

The overall-score regression was rebuilt independently with statsmodels (OLS, HC3, t inference) and agreed. Holm adjustment was rechecked with statsmodels. The five Firth fits were refitted in R 4.6.1 with brglm2 1.1.1 (mean bias reduction). Coefficients, covariance matrices, Wald intervals and p values agreed: the largest coefficient difference was 3.7e-07 and the largest covariance difference 8.5e-07. To converge with the small 'other' sex category, the R fit needed an intercept-only start and step size 0.25.

The code, run order and checks are documented in the repository; no participant-level rows are written.

## Source measures

New et al.: OSF project 2bdn6. The data file for Study 2 is named Exp3.1020.txt in the archive. The questionnaire (Study2.Questionnaires.pdf in the archive) is the 45-item QMI + VocEmo, New et al.'s extension of the Questionnaire upon Mental Imagery with vocal and emotional items. The six senses used here are its sight, sound, smell, taste, touch and movement scales. Its emotion, voice and inner-body scales are not in the overall score. New et al.'s attention rule keeps people who repeated at least three of their five favourite activities.

Arnold et al.: the public workbook labels its six PSI-Q columns visual, auditory, smell, taste, touch and bodily sensation. All 327 autistic records in Arnold et al.'s analysis code match the workbook on imagery scores, AQ and age, and all have the maximum attention score (13). How the comparison group was recruited is not documented in these files.

## Comparing the two datasets

The registered six-sense differences were -0.42 SD (New) and -0.18 SD (Arnold). Their difference, -0.24 SD, had a two-sided p of .11 under a normal approximation. This comparison treats the samples as independent and the two composites as comparable; neither is established, so we do not interpret it.
