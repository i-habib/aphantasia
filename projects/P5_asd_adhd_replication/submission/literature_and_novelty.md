## Novelty judgment

The broad autism-imagery association and smell/taste findings are already known. This project's contribution is testing their generalization in a different existing survey, with a registered analysis and a separate ADHD comparison. The lack of supported specificity narrows the contribution. The measurement mismatch reduces the strength of a strict replication claim but the five-sense check supports the broad average association. No new statistical method or neural mechanism is demonstrated.
